Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Publisher: GeesTech

The whole policy, in one sentence

Goobaley does not collect, store, or share any personal data, and it makes no internet connections — on Android it doesn't even have the permission.

What the app stores, and where

  • Player profiles — a display name you type, a chosen emoji avatar, and game progress (XP, coins, streaks, achievements, board skins, and settings such as language, theme, and sound) — are stored only on your device, in the operating system's standard app storage.
  • No account is created. No email address, phone number, password, or any other identifier is requested or stored.
  • Nothing leaves your device. On Android the app declares no internet permission and is excluded from cloud backup, so your game data is never uploaded anywhere. On iPhone, like any app's settings, it can be part of your own encrypted iCloud or computer backup if you use one — that copy is yours alone and is never sent to us.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking of any kind, across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo access.
  • No sale or sharing of data — there is no data to sell or share.

Device permissions

None. Goobaley asks for no device permissions. Gameplay haptics use your phone's built-in touch feedback; turn them off, together with sound, in Settings → Sound.

Children

Goobaley is a family-friendly game. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Deleting your data

Everything the app knows lives in its local storage. Deleting the app removes all of it. Inside the app, Settings → Delete profile removes a profile and all its progress, and Settings → Reset progress clears your XP, coins, streak, achievements and board skins (your profile name and avatar are kept so you can keep playing).

Changes

If a future version adds an online mode (playing a friend over the internet), this policy will be updated first, and any network feature will continue to work without accounts or personal identifiers.

Siyaasadda oo dhan, hal jumlad

Goobaley ma ururiso, ma kaydiso, mana wadaagto wax xog shakhsi ah, mana sameeyo xiriir internet — Android-ka xitaa fasaxa internet ma lahan.

Waxa uu app-ku kaydiyo, iyo halka

  • Astaanta ciyaartoyga — magac aad qortay, sawir emoji ah aad dooratay, iyo horumarka ciyaarta (XP, lacag, taxane, abaalmarinno, looxyo, iyo dejinta sida luqadda, midabka, iyo codka) — waxay ku kaydsan yihiin taleefankaaga oo keliya, kaydka caadiga ah ee app-ada.
  • Akoon lama sameeyo. Iimayl, lambar taleefan, furaha sirta ah, ama wax aqoonsi kale ah lama waydiiyo, lamana kaydiyo.
  • Waxba kama baxaan taleefankaaga. Android-ka app-ku ma lahan fasax internet, kamana mid aha kaydinta daruuraha (cloud backup) — xogtaada ciyaarta meelna looma diro. iPhone-ka, sida dejinta app kasta, xogtu waxay ku jiri kartaa kaydkaaga gaarka ah ee iCloud ama kombiyuutarka haddii aad isticmaasho — taasi adiga keliya ayay kuu gaar tahay, annagana nalooma soo diro.

Waxa uusan app-ku samayn

  • Ma jiro falanqayn (analytics), warbixin qalad, ama la-socod.
  • Ma jiro xayeysiis, mana jiro aqoonsi xayeysiis.
  • Ma jiro raad-raac nooc kasta ah, app-ada iyo websaydhada dhexdooda.
  • Ma jiro helitaan goob, xiriiriyeyaal, kaamero, makarafoon, ama sawirro.
  • Xog lama iibiyo, lamana wadaago — xog la iibiyo ma jirto.

Fasaxyada taleefanka

Midna. Goobaley ma weydiiso fasax taleefan. Dareenka ciyaarta wuxuu isticmaalaa dareenka caadiga ah ee taleefanka; waad ka damin kartaa, codka la jira, Dejinta → Codka.

Carruurta

Goobaley waa ciyaar qoys oo dhan ku habboon. Maadaama uusan cidna xog ka ururin, carruurtana xog kama ururiyo.

Tirtiridda xogtaada

Wax kasta oo app-ku og waxay ku jiraan kaydka taleefankaaga. Tirtiridda app-ka ayaa wada tirtirta. App-ka gudihiisa, Dejinta → Tirtir profile-ka waxay tirtirtaa profile-ka iyo horumarkiisa oo dhan, Dejinta → Dib-u-bilow horumarka -na waxay tirtirtaa XP-ga, lacagta, taxanaha, abaalmarinnada iyo looxyada (magacaaga iyo sawirkaagu way hadhayaan si aad u sii ciyaarto).
